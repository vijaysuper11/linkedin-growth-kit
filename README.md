# LinkedIn Growth Kit

An AI-agnostic kit to grow your LinkedIn profile, visibility and followers with genuine,
human-sounding engagement. You approve every comment and post, which keeps your account safe
and your voice authentic.

Inspired by the structure of markfulton/ai-employees (role folders, routines, "last click is yours" guardrails)
and sickn33/agentic-awesome-skills (portable SKILL.md playbooks and AGENTS.md compatibility).
Both are MIT-licensed; this kit is original content in that style.

## Install

- Claude (Code or Cowork): add this folder as a plugin. Skills load automatically.
- Codex, OpenCode, Cursor, Gemini CLI, Copilot: open the folder; the tool reads AGENTS.md and skills/*/SKILL.md.
- ChatGPT, Gemini web or any chat AI: paste universal/PROMPT.md into your instructions.

## First run
1. Fill config/owner-profile.template.md and save it as config/owner-profile.md.
2. Say: "Run voice-profile" and share 5 to 10 samples of your writing.
3. Say: "Run profile-optimizer".
4. Each day: "Run daily-engagement". Each week: "Run content-planner" and "Run weekly-review".

## Why draft-and-approve instead of auto-posting
LinkedIn forbids bots and automated activity, and restricts accounts that use them. Generic
auto-comments also damage your reputation. Approving a batch takes about 10 minutes a day.
