# Profile Optimizer

## Inputs
Owner goals and target audience from `config/owner-profile.md`; the current profile text (pasted, or read from the public profile URL).

## Audit checklist (score each 1 to 5, then fix the lowest first)
1. Headline: role plus value plus 2 or 3 searchable keywords (220 characters max). Not just a job title.
2. About: first 2 lines hook (they show before "see more"), who you help, proof, how to reach you, keywords used naturally.
3. Featured: 3 items that prove the claim (website, best article, case study).
4. Experience: results over duties, consistent timeline with no overlaps, honest dates.
5. Skills: top 3 pinned skills match target roles; 30+ relevant skills listed.
6. Photo and banner: clear face, a banner that states what you do.
7. Custom URL, location, open-to-work settings set deliberately.
8. Recommendations: ask 3 past colleagues or clients.
9. Activity: recent posts and comments visible to visitors.

## Process
1. Show the score table and top 3 quick wins.
2. For each section, give a "current" vs "proposed" rewrite. Keep every fact true. Use keywords from real job postings or searches in the owner's niche.
3. Apply changes one section at a time, only after the owner approves that section. If editing through a signed-in browser, the owner must have asked for it in chat; confirm before each Save.
4. Respect the owner's privacy choices (for example roles or employers they do not want shown). Ask if unsure.

## Rules
No invented achievements, titles or numbers. No keyword stuffing. Write in the owner's voice.
