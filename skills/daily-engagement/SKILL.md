# Daily Engagement Routine

## Before starting
Read `config/owner-profile.md`, `config/voice-profile.md`, `config/engagement-rules.md` and the last 7 days of `tracking/engagement-log.md` (avoid repeating the same people and phrasing).

## Steps
1. Source posts (use whichever the tool supports):
   - If the owner has a signed-in browser connected and has asked for it in this chat: open their LinkedIn feed in read-only mode and collect 10 to 15 recent posts from the niche. Do not click Like, Comment or Connect.
   - Otherwise ask the owner to paste 5 to 10 posts, or search the web for recent public posts from people listed in `engagement-rules.md`.
2. Filter. Keep posts that are: from relevant peers, recent (under 24 hours is best for reach), positive or educational, and where the owner has a genuine angle. Prefer posts with fewer than 100 comments so the comment is seen.
3. Draft using the `comment-drafter` skill. Aim for 8 to 12 drafts, a mix of established creators (weighted lower) and peers with smaller followings (weighted higher, where relationships form).
4. Add up to 3 connection suggestions: people whose posts the owner engaged with, each with a short personalised note (under 200 characters) referencing something specific. No pitching.
5. Write the queue to `tracking/engagement-log.md` under today's date with status `drafted`.
6. Present the queue and stop. Ask the owner which to approve.

## Owner approval
Items move to `approved` only when the owner says so. The owner posts them, spaced across the day. Update the log to `posted` when the owner confirms.

## Daily limits
15 comments, 5 connection requests. Fewer is fine. Quality beats volume.

## Timing guidance
Comment within the first hour or two of a post going live. Leave one or two thoughtful comments rather than many shallow ones. Reply to anyone who responds to the owner's comments the same day (draft those replies too).
