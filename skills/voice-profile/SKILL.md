# Voice Profile

## Goal
Create `config/voice-profile.md`, a short guide the other skills follow to write in the owner's voice.

## Steps
1. Ask the owner for 5 to 10 samples of their own writing: past LinkedIn posts or comments, emails, Quora answers, articles. If they have fewer, ask them to answer 3 short questions in their natural style (what they do, a lesson learned, a tool they like).
2. Analyse and record:
   - Sentence length and rhythm (short and punchy vs flowing)
   - Vocabulary level and regional spelling (for example Indian English or US spelling)
   - Warmth and humour level, formality, use of questions
   - Favourite phrases and openers, how they sign off
   - Emoji habits (none, rare, frequent) and punctuation habits
   - Topics they speak about with authority
3. List 10 "never say" phrases that would sound un-like them.
4. Write 3 sample comments in the voice on a neutral topic and ask the owner to rate each 1 to 5. Adjust.
5. Save the final guide to `config/voice-profile.md` (under 300 words).

## Rules
- Use only what the samples show. Do not invent personality traits.
- Keep imperfections that make the owner sound human (contractions, casual phrasing).
- Ask before saving. Re-run every few months.
