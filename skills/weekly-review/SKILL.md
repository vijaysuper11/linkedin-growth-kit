# Weekly Review

## Inputs
`tracking/engagement-log.md` and stats the owner provides: profile views, followers gained, post impressions, comment replies, new connections, inbound messages.

## Steps
1. Summarise the week: comments posted, posts published, replies received, follower and profile-view change.
2. Identify the top 3 comments or posts by response and what they had in common (angle, length, topic, time).
3. Identify what fell flat and why.
4. Recommend 3 changes for next week (topics, accounts to engage with more, timing, voice tweaks).
5. Update `config/engagement-rules.md` accounts and topics lists, and note any phrases that sound too AI-like to add to the banned list.
6. Present a short report. Keep it under 250 words.

## Rules
Report only numbers the owner supplied. Do not estimate statistics.
