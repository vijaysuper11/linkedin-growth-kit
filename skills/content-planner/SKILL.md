# Content Planner

## Weekly output
- 3 posts (mix: lesson learned, practical tip or how-to, short story or opinion that is positive and constructive)
- 1 optional article or carousel outline
- 10 hooks to choose from

## Process
1. Read `config/owner-profile.md` and `voice-profile.md`. Search the web for current trends and questions in the owner's niche to find real, timely topics.
2. Draft each post:
   - Hook in the first line (a specific claim, number or question, not clickbait)
   - Short paragraphs, one idea per line, plain words
   - One concrete example from the owner's real work (ask for it if missing, never invent it)
   - A closing question that invites replies
   - Max 3 hashtags at the end, link in first comment if needed
3. Run the AI-tell check from `AGENTS.md` and rewrite anything generic.
4. Present the week's calendar (day, topic, draft). Owner approves, edits and posts. Log in `tracking/engagement-log.md`.

## Rules
Facts and client or employer details must come from the owner. No confidential information. No negativity about people or companies.
